ABOUT THE STUDIO

DESIGN WITH
A PURPOSE.

Leonardo Graphics & Artworks is a small design practice in Kiribathgoda, Sri Lanka. I make posts, product labels, banners and wedding cards that look considered and feel right in the hand.

Every project starts with a conversation and ends with files you can actually use. No jargon, no noise — just clear decisions, careful craft and work that earns its place.
